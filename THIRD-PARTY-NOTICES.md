## Assets and external services

- The PNG add-in icons are reproducibly generated by
  `addin/scripts/generate-icons.mjs`; they are repository-native geometric artwork.
- Public demo inputs use fictitious people and `example.com` addresses.
- Original mailbox screenshots and AI-edited LinkedIn images are excluded from
  the public source. Only inspected synthetic application captures belong in docs.
- Office.js is loaded at runtime from Microsoft's hosted CDN, not redistributed
  here. Outlook/Microsoft 365, model services and downloaded model weights have
  their own terms; this license does not cover those products.
- Gitleaks 8.30.1 (MIT) is downloaded for verification only. Its executable and
  documentation are ignored build artifacts, not source deliverables.
